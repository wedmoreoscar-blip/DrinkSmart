description: Update the durable decision ledger after a genuine design, architecture, benchmark, or workflow change. Use when asked to log, record, checkpoint, supersede, or update a project decision.
---

1. Read `docs/decisions.md` in full.
2. Record only genuine settled decisions, explicit pending choices, supersessions, or
   benchmark-backed findings. Do not turn ordinary discussion into ledger churn.
3. Append or amend using the existing `LOCKED`, `PENDING`, or `SUPERSEDED` structure. Preserve history.
4. If nothing qualifies, make no edits and say so.
