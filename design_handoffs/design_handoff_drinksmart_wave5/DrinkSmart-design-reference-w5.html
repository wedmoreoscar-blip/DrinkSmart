5xThe three decisions these four screens make, and one thing I would raise
Curating a night needs one new control class and no new colour. The class is a plain text control — hide / show — the first thing in the set with neither outline nor fill.
1 · The text control. 19px, accent-light, no border, no fill, sitting in a 56px target that is invisible until pressed (pressed = accent tint at 10% behind the label, radius 8). It is legible as a control because it is the only accent-coloured text in the row and it is right-aligned into empty space. It is deliberately weaker than the chevron beside it: the chevron opens the category screen, the text collapses the list, and the two never share a target.
2 · Two controls on a small repeating row. Lock and delete are 56 × 56 icon-only targets with no box, at the trailing edge, and the row pays for them in text, not in height: the price leaves its own column and joins the 13px meta line (pint · 22.7 ml · £4.55). One line of name, one line of meta, two silent glyphs. No dividers between the controls, no background — that is what would make it a toolbar.
3 · What yields at 64px on the Timeline (5b). The time column narrows 62 → 56 (a tabular 23:10 at 19px is 48px wide, so nothing is lost) and the row's meta collapses to one line, absorbing the ml figure. Lock stays where 1d put it — trailing, outermost — and swap takes the slot inside it, so a lock never moves as rows gain or lose swap. On the highlighted current row the same pair applies; its 13px third line folds into the sub.
4 · Three controls fit, but not three at the trailing edge. The measurement is why: 362 − 56 time − 34 marker − 3 × 56 leaves 104px of content, and Camden Hells at 22px is 140px — a third trailing control costs the drink's name or the 19px floor. So the reorder control goes where the row already has dead space: a 44 × 36 grip in the time column, directly under the clock, left of the spine. The clock is 48px wide and 24px tall in a 56 × 74 column; the grip costs 10px of row height and nothing horizontal. Press and hold it to lift the drink (5e). It is the one control drawn under 56px square — deliberately, and only because a press-and-hold cannot be triggered by a brush the way a tap can; its width is the full column and the whole row becomes the drag surface once held. If you would rather pay 20px of row height for a 56 × 56 grip, that is the trade and it is yours to make.
5 · The affordance is absent, never inert. Only a future, unlocked entry has a grip: past rows, the drink up next and locked drinks have none — and in move mode a locked row keeps its solid padlock in the trailing slot, which is the reason it cannot be dragged, stated where the grip would be. The footer, with Add a drink gone, is one full-width 56px Re-plan the rest: a single narrow button in a row built for a pair reads like something is missing, and a 64px full-width one would compete with Had it in the hero. The 13px line above it carries the discovery — Press and hold a grip to move that drink.
One thing to raise, not encoded here. In 5c the +20% bound is drawn as absence: ineligible drinks are not in the list, the bound is stated once at the top, and a 13px footnote counts what is missing. Greying them would advertise the stronger drink you cannot have on a screen whose whole job is a like-for-like replacement. The risk is a user who came looking for a specific stronger drink and cannot see why it is gone — the footnote is what carries that, and it is the weakest part of these four. If you would rather they were listed and disabled, that is a one-row change and I would not fight it.
