Same panel as the dialog, anchored to the bottom and rounded on the top two corners only (20px). It is dismissable, so it carries the affordances the dialog refuses: a 44×4 grab handle, backdrop tap, and swipe-down. That difference is the whole distinction between the two — a sheet with no handle would be lying.

Five stops, each with a word — but only the chosen word is shown, at 22px. Five words across 402px would land at 11px and be unreadable, which is how a word scale usually fails. The ends are anchored in micro; the middle is read by position.

Two columns, 15-minute steps, 56px rows — the selection band is fixed and the numbers move under it. Nobody sets a start time to the minute on a night out, so the minute column has four stops, not sixty. Now is the answer most of the time and is one tap.
No new tokens. All four are built from what 1l and 1k already declare — the sheet is the dialog panel bottom-anchored at --radius-sheet, the radio is the checkbox at --touch-control in a round well, the picker rows are --touch-min, and the toast is a card at --radius. That the set closed without additions is the point.
