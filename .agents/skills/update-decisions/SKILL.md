name: update-decisions
description: Update the durable decision ledger and coding-agent roster after a genuine design, architecture, benchmark, workflow, or role-model change. Use when asked to log, record, checkpoint, supersede, or update a project decision or agent role.
---

1. Read `docs/decisions.md` and `docs/agent_roster.md` in full.
2. Record only genuine settled decisions, explicit pending choices, supersessions, benchmark-backed
   findings, or material role changes. Do not turn ordinary discussion into ledger churn.
3. Append or amend using the existing `LOCKED`, `PENDING`, or `SUPERSEDED` structure. Preserve history.
4. Update the matching roster row only when its model, responsibility, tools, or rationale changed.
5. If nothing qualifies, make no edits and say so.
