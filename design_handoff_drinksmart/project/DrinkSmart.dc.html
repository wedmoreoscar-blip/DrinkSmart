1DrinkSmart — visual language, token layer, and the screens that matter
1aAssumptions, context, reasoning
The app is a bar instrument, not a screen. One hand, one thumb, a dark loud room, and a user who is less sober every hour. Everything below follows from that; nothing below is decoration.
Read from the repo

index.css and tailwind.config.ts (stock Lovable set — vibrant purple, hot pink, amber, lavender ground), Dashboard.tsx (top tabs, centred title), PlanTab.tsx (1–10 buzz slider, duration slider + presets), TimelineTab.tsx and SortableTimelineItem.tsx, the battery meter at DrinksTab.tsx:878, and data/buzzLevels.ts. Every figure shown is from that source: Carling 4%, House Red 12%, 25 ml vodka shot, 568 ml pint, Watson TBW, £.

The system, in five rules

One accent, no palette — blurple as a line, a level, and a now-marker. No red, no green — one amber means over-target; completion desaturates instead of celebrating. 19px floor for anything legible; no thin greys. 56px floor on every target, 64px on the one action per screen. Slow motion — liquid settles over 900ms, nothing flashes or bounces.

Departures from Nocturne

Its ground, mono accent, tonal ramps, radii and Inter/500 headings carry over intact, and its fading-rule signature becomes the timeline spine. Its 0.70× density and 15px body do not survive: they are a liability at arm's length, half-drunk. The type and touch scales are DrinkSmart's own. I also dropped the light theme — a light mode in a dark bar is a flashbang.

Your notes, answered

The notification is designed as a screen (1g), not an afterthought. Water and breaks are a first-class row type — hollow marker, a duration, no ml (1d). Wind-down is its own state with a sober-by estimate (1f). Locked drinks and unplanned additions read as adjustment, never breakage. Nothing anywhere says you succeeded.

Assumptions — shoot these down

Bands map to buzzLevels as Light 1–2, Social 3–4, Loose 5–6, with a softer/stronger nudge inside a band; levels 7–10 do not exist in the UI, so the danger warning has nothing to warn about. Profile is 82 kg / 180 cm / 28 / male, 4 h from 21:30. Sober-by uses 0.015%/h elimination.

Not designed yet

Profile / onboarding (StatsForm, PreferencesPicker), the drink picker and menu scanner, establishment browsing, auth. Say the word and they follow the same tokens. The engine work for a 0 ml break row is yours — the row design assumes duration, not ethanol, drives it.
